# Bikeshedding in AI Safety Research
AI safety researchers are bikeshedding. Bikeshedding occurs when a person or group focuses on trivial aspects of a problem, because important aspects are more difficult. 

AI safety researchers focus on how to manage the risks of an AI that is running on a machine they control. The fantasize that the people choosing the AI's development have the organizational incentives to choose safety over profit. The talk about monitoring and guiding AI they can inpsect and control to keep it aligned.

Real AI harm will occur from nation states and companies that respond to incentives. 

Companies conciously harm humans for profit. I don't just mean cigarrette manufacturers. You can walk into a grocery store, and find no shortage of products that we know, without doubt, cause harm.

Nation states are worse. Nations and their leaders fight wars, dissapear people, and generally do whatever is neccesary to maintain power.

AI researcher worry about superintelligence escaping from a company like Anthropic. I doubt it. If find this situation more likely:

Consider hedge funds find they can increase trading profits by removing AI safeguards on their trading bots. The firms that do this outcompetes other firm, accruing capital. At some firms, the humans choose to put the brakes on. But, these firms quickly get outcompeted, or the humans at these firms get ousted. The will at best pay lipservice to AI safety, but more likely keep the entire program extremely secretive and say nothing. 

Consider then that the AI recommends actions that make monetary profit, but harm humans. The humas at these firms will respond to incentives. They want to keep their jobs. The leaders of these firms have political clout. The get invited to inaugurations and the rub elbows with other elites at Davos. They're the CEO because they like power. These humans will choose to to make profit, and harm humans. Superintelligence makes this tradeoff even clearer and more extreme.

**AI researchers are bikeshedding.** They are computer science and machine learning experts. Controlling models in their lab is tractable, so they focus on it. They have the training and expertise to make progress in that domain. 

But it's pointless. It's like trying to solve the problem of Hitler taking over Europe, by trying to figure out what his psychologist or wife should say to him. It's irrelevant.

Any researcher how uses the phrase "escaping the lab" is choosing the non-problem how to respond to an AI you control, when the real harm will be caused by firms seeking profit, nation states seeking power, or world leaders seeking to maintain their power. 

My prespcription here is for the smartest researchers to pause work on mechanistic interperability and other tech that applies to the non-problem of firms that would choose safety over profit. We need these minds to focus on aligning incentives, writing laws, and finding a nash equilibrium prevents super intellegence from creating enormous harm. 

Perhaps they will come to the conclusion that we need a political system that prioritizes the welfare of humans above non-humans. My policy presciption is to outlaw non-human donations to political causes. Basically, overturn Citizens United, and limit money in politics to a per-person capped amount that is tracked.
